Dojo Etiquette & Gym Rules

Respect the Mat

Bow when stepping on and off the mat. Arrive on time and greet your instructor and training partners.

Kit & Hygiene

Wear a clean gi or training kit. Trim nails, remove jewellery, and keep feet clean before stepping onto the mats.

Train Safely

Control your technique with training partners. No unsupervised sparring, and always tap early to avoid injury.

Gym Courtesy

Re-rack weights, wipe down equipment after use, and keep noise respectful of classes in session.
